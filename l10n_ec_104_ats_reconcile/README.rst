This module reconciles the Form 104 aggregates of one month against the
ATS blocks of the same month: Σ ``ventasEstab`` (net per establishment)
against the 104's 419, and Σ ``montoIva`` (absolute per row) against the
104's 429 rebuilt gross with twice the credit-note tax.

The gross/net split is the whole point: ATS ``totalVentas`` sums
credit-note magnitudes while the 104 nets them, so a direct comparison
drifts by twice the notes every month with returns. A drift names its
amounts on both sides; a period the ATS cannot build is refused with its
reasons rather than reconciled partially.

Configuration
=============

Nothing to configure.

The module requires ``l10n_ec_104`` and ``l10n_ec_ats`` to be installed.
The only user interface is the reconciliation wizard, reachable from
**Invoicing ‣ SRI ‣ 104 vs ATS**.

Usage
=====

Reconciling a month:

``l10n.ec.104.ats.reconcile`` takes a company, a year and a month, runs
both collectors over the same posted moves, and reports one verdict per
check.

In the interface it is **Invoicing ‣ SRI ‣ 104 vs ATS**, a dialog asking
for the company, the year and the month with a single **Reconcile**
button. The button offers a CSV download and renders the verdict on
screen: ``MATCH``, or ``DRIFT`` with the ATS and 104 amounts side by
side.

A period the ATS cannot build (an unfilable document, a catalog gap)
arrives in **one** ``UserError`` with the ATS reasons, and no verdict is
produced. Reconciling against a partial ATS side would bless a file the
company never had.
