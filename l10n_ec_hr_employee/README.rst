This module adds the fields *Firstname* and *Lastname* to the employee
form and keeps them concatenated in the *Name* field.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

On the employee form, you have two separate fields, *Firstname* and
*Lastname*, both required. The *Name* field is computed from them.
