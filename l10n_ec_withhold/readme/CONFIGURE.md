To configure this module, you need to:

1. **Related Party on the vendor:** the SRI *Comprobante de Retención* reports
   whether the withheld subject is a related party (`parteRel`). Set **Related
   Party** on the partner form next to the withholding tax support fields. It
   defaults to unchecked, which the certificate reports as `NO`; check it to
   report `SI`.
