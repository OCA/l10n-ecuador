Ecuadorian SRI Form 104 (IVA)

This module pre-fills the boxes of the SRI monthly IVA return (Formulario 104) that derive from the posted moves of the reported month, with traceability from every prefilled box back to its documents.

It does not reimplement what Odoo core already computes: the 104 report of l10n_ec (engine tax_tags, ~110 lines) already derives the sales/purchase summaries, the 5% materials boxes, the 423/424 and 526/527 adjustments and the perception/withholding frame. Those boxes are read, never recalculated. What this module adds is the layer core does not ship: the variable-rate boxes (410/420/430, 530/533/534), the credit-sale settlement (480-485/499), the slip counts (111/113/115/117/119), the proportionality factor (563) with its suggested credit (564/565), and the wizard that composes them.

Configuration

Nothing to configure.

The module requires l10n_ec_base and account to be installed. The only user interface is the prefill wizard, reachable from Invoicing ‣ SRI ‣ Form 104 (IVA).

Usage

Prefilling a month:

l10n.ec.104.generate takes a company, a year and a month, and pre-fills every derivable box of that month’s Form 104 from its posted moves: the sales block (401-454), the purchase block (500-560) and the settlement (480-499, 563-565, 601-699, 799-859).

In the interface it is Invoicing ‣ SRI ‣ Form 104 (IVA), a dialog asking for the company, the year and the month with a single Prefill button. The button offers a CSV download (formulario_104_YYYYMM.csv) and renders the same boxes on screen. The filing itself happens in the SRI portal: carry the CSV values over, review the suggested boxes (563/564/565 and any carryover you filled), and complete the manual boxes there.

Prior-period boxes (prior 485, prior credit balances, compensations and adjustments) are not derived: fill the matching carryover_* fields from your prior filing before pressing Prefill. They default to zero and are reported as carried, never as computed.

Everything that cannot be derived arrives in one UserError, numbered, and no file is produced. A company filing outside Ecuador is refused the same way. Nothing is computed and nothing is assumed on refusal.

Known issues / Roadmap

Roadmap

Out of scope in v1, with rationale and a v2 proposal. Each entry states the evidence and what a future version would need.

Boxes Why out of v1 Evidence v2 proposal
Prior-month balances and carryovers (605-608 carried, 615/617/618/619 carried, 483) Inter-period state, not derivable from the month’s moves Form boxes 605-608, 615, 617-619, 483 l10n_ec_104_balances glue reading prior filings + withholdings received (609)
Payments, sustitutivas, interest/fines (890, 897/898/899, 902/903/904/999, 880) Treasury/post-filing, not accounting Form boxes 890, 897-899, 902-904, 999, 880 l10n_ec_104_payment glue
Compensations and sector benefits (603, 604, 607/608, 610-614, 622, 624/625) Require case files and resolutions, user assertion Form boxes 603-604, 607-608, 610-614, 622, 624-625 Manual boxes with audit trail, never derived
Merger/absorption (623, 492) Requires mercantile-registry deed, one-off event Form boxes 623, 492 Manual box gated on deed date
ISD refund for exporters (700/701/702) Needs customs/FUE data model absent from Odoo Form boxes 700-702 Export document model (shared gap with ATS)
Unpaid withholdings, public sector (802) Sector-specific settlement state Form box 802 Manual box for public entities
COVID deferred payment (881-887), presumptive gambling (621) Expired/special regimes with own registries Form boxes 881-887, 621 Only on explicit maintainer decision
Tourism variable rate (203) Needs executive decree + Tourism Ministry cadastre Form box 203 Rate table resolved for the reported period
RIMPE semestrial filing Period rule (June/December under code 2011), not extra boxes SRI guide, RIMPE regime Wizard period option, no new boxes
Informative reimbursements (434/444/454, 535/545/555), non-object/exempt (431/441, 531/541, 532/542) Informative-only in v1, no own apportionment Form boxes 431, 434, 531-532, 535 Reported as-is; apportionment only if the SRI requires it

Reconciliation of box 429 against the ATS totalVentas header is a separate follow-up (l10n_ec_104_ats_reconcile), never a condition of this module: the ATS derives totalVentas from its ventas rows while the 104 reads 429 from tags, and wiring them together here would couple this PR to the open ATS PR (#105).
