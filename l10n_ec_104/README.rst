This module pre-fills the boxes of the SRI monthly IVA return
(Formulario 104) that derive from the posted moves of the reported
month, with traceability from every prefilled box back to its documents.

It does not reimplement what Odoo core already computes: the 104 report
of ``l10n_ec`` (engine ``tax_tags``, ~110 lines) already derives the
sales/purchase summaries, the 5% materials boxes, the 423/424 and
526/527 adjustments and the perception/withholding frame. Those boxes
are read, never recalculated. What this module adds is the layer core
does not ship: the variable-rate boxes (410/420/430, 530/533/534), the
credit-sale settlement (480-485/499), the slip counts
(111/113/115/117/119), the proportionality factor (563) with its
suggested credit (564/565), and the wizard that composes them.

Configuration
=============

Nothing to configure.

The module requires ``l10n_ec_base`` and ``account`` to be installed.
The only user interface is the prefill wizard, reachable from
**Invoicing ‣ SRI ‣ Form 104 (IVA)**.

Usage
=====

Prefilling a month:

``l10n.ec.104.generate`` takes a company, a year and a month, and
pre-fills every derivable box of that month's Form 104 from its posted
moves: the sales block (401-454), the purchase block (500-560) and the
settlement (480-499, 563-565, 601-699, 799-859).

In the interface it is **Invoicing ‣ SRI ‣ Form 104 (IVA)**, a dialog
asking for the company, the year and the month with a single **Prefill**
button. The button offers a CSV download (``formulario_104_YYYYMM.csv``)
and renders the same boxes on screen. The filing itself happens in the
SRI portal: carry the CSV values over, review the suggested boxes
(563/564/565 and any carryover you filled), and complete the manual
boxes there.

Prior-period boxes (prior 485, prior credit balances, compensations and
adjustments) are not derived: fill the matching ``carryover_*`` fields
from your prior filing before pressing Prefill. They default to zero and
are reported as carried, never as computed.

Everything that cannot be derived arrives in **one** ``UserError``,
numbered, and no file is produced. A company filing outside Ecuador is
refused the same way. Nothing is computed and nothing is assumed on
refusal.

Reconciliation of box 429 against the ATS ``totalVentas`` header is a
separate follow-up (``l10n_ec_104_ats_reconcile``), never a condition of
this module: the ATS derives ``totalVentas`` from its ``ventas`` rows
while the 104 reads 429 from tags, and wiring them together here would
couple this PR to the open ATS PR (#105).
