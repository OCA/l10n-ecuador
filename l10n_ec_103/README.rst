This module pre-fills the boxes of the SRI monthly withholding return
(Formulario 103) that derive from the posted moves of the reported
month, with traceability from every prefilled box back to its documents.

It does not reimplement what Odoo core already computes: the 103 report
of ``l10n_ec`` (engine ``tax_tags``) already derives the resident and
non-resident concept lines. Those boxes are read, never recalculated.
What this module adds is the layer core does not ship: the subtotals
(349/399, 497/498), the totals (499/501), the concepto→valor upload
file, and the wizard that composes them with the manual boxes (payroll,
dividends, special regimes, payments).

Configuration
=============

Nothing to configure.

The module requires ``l10n_ec_base`` and ``account`` to be installed.
The only user interface is the prefill wizard, reachable from
**Invoicing ‣ SRI ‣ Form 103 (Withholding)**.

Usage
=====

Prefilling a month:

``l10n.ec.103.generate`` takes a company, a year and a month, and
pre-fills every derivable box of that month's Form 103 from its posted
moves: the resident block, the non-resident block and the totals.

In the interface it is **Invoicing ‣ SRI ‣ Form 103 (Withholding)**, a
dialog asking for the company, the year and the month with a single
**Prefill** button. The button offers a CSV download
(``formulario_103_YYYYMM.csv``) with concepto→valor pairs -- the shape
the SRI XML/JSON upload guide asks for -- and renders the same boxes on
screen. The filing itself happens in the SRI portal: carry the values
over, review the 500 compensation you filled, and complete the manual
boxes there.

The 500 compensation (undistributed-profit advance, art. 39.2.1 LRTI) is
not derived: fill ``carryover_500`` from your dividends reading before
pressing Prefill. It defaults to zero and is reported as carried, never
as computed.

Everything that cannot be derived arrives in **one** ``UserError``,
numbered, and no file is produced. A company filing outside Ecuador is
refused the same way. Nothing is computed and nothing is assumed on
refusal.
